About this chart

An arsenal works best when each ball covers something the others don't. The ladder ranks every ball on one reaction scale, from light to heavy, so you can see where the balls you throw sit, which ones overlap, and the gaps where another ball would add something new.

Oil Volume
How much oil the ball is made for, from light to heavy. Rated 1 to 11; each ball sits at the middle of its range.

Rank by another scale from the menu above the ladder. To see two scales at once, use the reaction chart.
